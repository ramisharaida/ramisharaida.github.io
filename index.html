Biography

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Engineering at the University of California, Irvine. My research interests lie at the intersection of cybersecurity and artificial intelligence, with a particular interest in LLM-based systems, AI agent security and privacy, network security, and explainable AI.

I received my B.Sc. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from the Islamic University of Technology, Bangladesh. My undergraduate research focused on photonic crystal fiber surface plasmon resonance sensing. Before graduate study, I worked as a Global Graduate in Operations at British American Tobacco Bangladesh.
